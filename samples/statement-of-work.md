# Statement of Work: Northwind Logistics warehouse data migration

Prepared for Northwind Logistics, contact dana.whitfield@northwind-logistics.example.

## Scope
The consultancy will migrate inventory records from the legacy AS/400 warehouse system to the new cloud ERP.
The migration covers 14 warehouses and roughly 2.3 million SKU records.
A one-day data migration workshop will be held on Oct 14, 2026 with the client's operations team.

## Deliverables
Data mapping specification, delivered by 2026-10-21.
Validated test migration of two pilot warehouses, delivered by 2026-11-04.
Production cutover runbook and hypercare plan, delivered by 2026-11-25.

## Commercials
Fixed fee of $42,000.00 billed in three milestones.
Workshop fee of $3,200.00 billed once after the workshop.
Payment terms are net 30 from invoice date.

## Risks
Legacy data quality is unknown; the client must provide a data owner for each warehouse.
Cutover requires a 12-hour freeze of warehouse transactions, to be scheduled by the client.
